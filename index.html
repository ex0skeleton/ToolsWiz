BEHIND THE TOOLS

A problem first.
A tool, eventually.

Built by Niaz Morshed
Marketer by profession. Maker by necessity.

Every tool here started with a specific problem I ran into. Instead of working around it, I tried building something to solve it.

They became part of my own toolkit. I still use them from time to time for work, and this is their little home on the internet. If they make your day a bit easier too, even better.
